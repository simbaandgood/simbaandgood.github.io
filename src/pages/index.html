Our story

It started with a small white dog named Simba.

Simba is my Bichon Frise — healthy, spoiled, and the reason this company exists. Caring for him well meant staying on top of a hundred small things at the right time, and it made me wonder why that was so hard. It also made the gap impossible to ignore: the strays and rescues with no one keeping track of any of it. Simba & Good is the answer to both — better care for the pets we love, and real support for the ones still waiting for someone.
