Simba is a Bichon Frise, and the reason this company exists. Caring for him well meant staying on top of a hundred small things at the right time &mdash; and it made us wonder why that was so hard, and why more of what pet owners already spend couldn't help the animals who have no one.

## Care that grows up with them

A puppy, an adult, and a senior dog need completely different things &mdash; and a Great Dane ages nothing like a Chihuahua. Simba & Good learns your dog's breed and age and tells you what matters at each stage, so you're never guessing.

## Doing good, built in

Every membership helps fund rescue, veterinary care, and rehoming. The dream is to give away the large majority of our profits to animal welfare &mdash; and to show, in the open, exactly how we get there.
